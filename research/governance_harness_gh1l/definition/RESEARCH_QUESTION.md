# GH-1L research question and claim boundary

GH-1L asks whether a Completed Experience, re-admitted only when a current-flow gap
exists and re-evaluated against the current relationship, causally changes later
decisions in a continuous long-horizon world whose relevant relationship history is
not recoverable from the current observation alone.

The object of study is the complete chain: current flow, structural gap, permission to
read history, candidate retrieval, context-local participation, participants-only Core
input, one selected possibility, one realization, and Closure provenance.

The experiment does **not** claim that memory, retrieval gating, provenance, or
long-term context is unique to OASIS. It does not test the responsibility mechanism,
real-world CARLA performance, or general superiority. A null G1/G3, G1/G4, or G1/G5
difference remains a valid result.

neither repaired nor pooled with GH-1L.
