# GH-4 Literature / Prior-Art Boundary

Status: STAGE_4_REVIEW_COMPLETE

This review is used to delimit the GH-4 experimental claim. It is not a patent novelty opinion.

## Reflexion — Shinn et al., 2023, arXiv:2303.11366

Reflexion stores feedback-derived linguistic reflections in episodic memory and reuses them in later trials without model-weight updates.

Boundary to GH-4: GH-4 does not test verbal self-reflection, prompt memory or task-level pass-rate improvement. It tests an explicit governance ordering around current-flow Gap gating, relation/context-specific participation, candidate-bound responsibility, irreversible single realization, authoritative Closure, typed provenance-bound revalidation and delayed eligibility of newly completed experiences.

## Generative Agents — Park et al., 2023, arXiv:2304.03442

Generative Agents maintain experience records, retrieve memories dynamically and synthesize higher-level reflections for planning.

Boundary to GH-4: GH-4 does not use importance/recency/relevance memory scoring as the governing principle and does not synthesize reflective summaries. A retrieved experience must separately pass a current contextual participation judgment. A REVISED state is contextual evidence rather than a global memory score.

## Voyager — Wang et al., 2023, arXiv:2305.16291

Voyager performs open-ended lifelong learning with an ever-growing executable skill library and environment-feedback/self-verification loops.

Boundary to GH-4: GH-4 does not learn or compose executable skills and does not claim open-ended competence growth. Its growing archive contains completed decision experiences and provenance. The experiment isolates governance continuity and later re-participation rather than skill acquisition.

## MemGPT — Packer et al., 2023, arXiv:2310.08560

MemGPT manages memory tiers and context movement to support long-running interactions beyond a fixed context window.

Boundary to GH-4: GH-4 is not a context-window or paging architecture. Archive access is triggered by current-flow Gap and is followed by explicit participation decisions, responsibility binding, realization and post-outcome revalidation.

## Related interactive-agent feedback literature

ReAct and related closed-loop agent work combine reasoning/action with environment feedback. Later reflection and planning systems use post-action observations to revise trajectories.

Boundary to GH-4: the GH-4 claim is narrower and architectural. It requires explicit separation of retrieval from participation, responsibility from possibility formation, realization from outcome, and revalidation from later eligibility, with provenance preserved through each transition.

## Claim discipline after review

GH-4 must not claim that long-term memory, reflection, feedback reuse, continual learning, or lifelong agents are novel categories. The experiment can support only the narrower proposition that the Governance OASIS causal layers can be integrated in one persistent long-horizon execution under the frozen rules and that prespecified ablations separate contributions within that synthetic system.
