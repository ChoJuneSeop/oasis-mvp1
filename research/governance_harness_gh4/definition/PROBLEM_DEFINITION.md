# GH-4 Problem Definition — Integrated Long-Horizon Governance

Status: STAGE_1_DEFINED

## Research question

Can one continuous Governance OASIS execution preserve the full causal order below over a long horizon without reset, future leakage, permanent memory weighting, or post-result tuning?

current reality observation
→ current-flow Gap judgment
→ Gap-gated archive access
→ current-context selective re-participation of decision-eligible Completed Experiences
→ current possibility formation
→ current U/I/V/T responsibility binding
→ exactly one realization
→ authoritative post-observation
→ Closure
→ typed revalidation
→ atomic commit of history + provenance + feedback + newly completed experience
→ only a later epoch may consider that newly completed experience again

## What GH-4 newly opens

GH-1/GH-1L validated selective historical re-participation with a frozen archive. GH-2 validated responsibility binding with history held empty. GH-3 validated outcome-based revalidation while newly committed Completed Experiences remained decision-ineligible.

GH-4 is the first experiment in this lineage to permit a Completed Experience created during the experimental run to become decision-eligible at a later epoch, while simultaneously retaining selective participation, responsibility binding, single realization, Closure, revalidation and provenance.

## Primary scientific target

The primary target is integrated causal continuity, not overall task superiority. GH-4 asks whether the separated mechanisms can remain mutually consistent while the archive grows and prior outcomes can change later participation.

## Non-claims

GH-4 does not claim:

- real-world or CARLA deployment validity,
- universal performance superiority,
- learned reward optimization,
- permanent experience weights or scalar memory scores,
- a universal outcome evaluator,
- that more history is always better,
- that every Completed Experience must re-participate,
- statistical population generalization from the finite synthetic matrix.

## Immutable causal prohibitions

1. No future observation may enter Gap, re-participation, possibility, responsibility or realization computation.
2. A newly committed Completed Experience may not be decision-eligible in the same epoch that created it.
3. Retrieval is not participation.
4. Revalidation state is provenance-bound evidence, not a permanent global weight.
5. REVISED is context-local and must not become permanent deletion or global exclusion.
6. Responsibility is recomputed from the current epoch and is not carried forward as a fixed scalar.
7. Exactly one selected possibility is realized per decision epoch.
8. Pilot results cannot alter the confirmatory horizon, arm definitions, scenario matrix, operator rules or evaluator.
