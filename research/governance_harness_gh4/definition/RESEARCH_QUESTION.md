# GH-4 v1.0 Research Question

Status: STAGE_1_DEFINED

GH-4 tests whether the already separated Governance OASIS causal layers can remain jointly coherent across a continuous multi-relation horizon without reset:

current flow -> Gap gate -> selective Completed Experience re-participation -> current U/I/V/T responsibility binding -> exactly one realization -> authoritative post-observation -> Closure -> typed revalidation -> provenance-bound feedback -> later current flow.

Primary question: can this complete governance loop preserve its causal contracts while newly committed Completed Experiences become eligible for later relation-local re-participation?

GH-4 does not re-score or re-rank GH-1/GH-1L, GH-2, or GH-3. It does not claim real-world superiority, prediction accuracy, universal optimality, or CARLA generalization.

The new evidence target is integration and long-horizon continuity, including the boundary that GH-1L and GH-3 deliberately left unvalidated: later reuse of newly committed Completed Experiences.
