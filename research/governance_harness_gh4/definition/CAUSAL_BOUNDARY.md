# GH-4 v1.0 Causal Boundary

Status: STAGE_2_3_FROZEN

GH-4 integrates three already separated layers without redefining them:

1. Selective re-participation: archive is accessed only after a current-flow Gap; among relation-local candidates, participation is re-decided in current context.
2. Responsibility-centered governance: U/I/V/T is recomputed from the current observation after actual possibilities exist and is bound to exactly one realization; no scalar responsibility score exists.
3. Outcome-based revalidation: only authoritative post-observation after realization can create typed CONFIRMED/REVISED/INCONCLUSIVE feedback.

Newly committed Completed Experiences may become decision-eligible only at a later epoch, never in the same epoch that created them. They carry provenance and are not given permanent weights. At most one relation-local experience participates per Gap in the frozen GH-4 selection policy; nonparticipation is contextual and preserved in audit.

Controls:
- L1_FULL: growing archive + committed feedback exposed.
- L2_FEEDBACK_RECORD_ONLY: growing archive + feedback committed but hidden from later participation decisions.
- L3_FROZEN_ARCHIVE: only initial prehistory is decision-eligible; newly committed Completed Experiences remain provenance evidence but cannot participate.

Responsibility and Core contracts are identical across all arms. The controls isolate long-horizon feedback and accumulation boundaries rather than re-running GH-2 as a winner comparison.

Forbidden: future information, evaluator labels in workers, same-epoch CE reuse, permanent scalar memory weights, global REVISED blacklist, post-result tuning, pilot-derived sample size, aggregate winner score.
