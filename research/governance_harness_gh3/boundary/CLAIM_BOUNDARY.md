# Stage 6 — GH-3 claim and interpretation boundary

## What a successful GH-3 may support
Within the frozen synthetic governance contract, GH-3 may support only the following bounded claims:

1. A post-realization governance judgment can be revalidated only after authoritative host outcome observation and Closure.
2. The revalidation can remain typed (`CONFIRMED / REVISED / INCONCLUSIVE`) and separately attributable to prior Gap, participation/nonparticipation, choice, and responsibility judgments.
3. Correct relation/provenance-bound revalidation feedback can have a causal effect on a later matched governance epoch when the later current observation, archive, Core, possibility formation, and responsibility contract are held fixed.
4. Withholding the same recorded feedback or mismatching its provenance can be used as causal controls.
5. This can be done without model-weight updates, reward/value fitting, permanent memory weights, same-epoch reuse, or changing the completed historical record.

## What GH-3 may not support
Even if every frozen test passes, GH-3 does not establish:

- general AI safety or performance superiority;
- that learning from outcomes is novel;
- that reflection, episodic memory, feedback learning, continual learning, or post-deployment monitoring are novel concepts;
- real-world deployment validity;
- correctness of all possible revalidation mappings outside the frozen scenarios;
- that a `REVISED` judgment should permanently suppress an experience/action;
- that a `CONFIRMED` judgment should be repeated automatically;
- long-term adaptation from newly committed Completed Experience reuse;
- recovery after a bad revalidation policy;
- human/legal responsibility attribution;
- an optimal governance policy.

## Relationship to earlier GH experiments
- GH-1: whether historical Completed Experience can be selectively re-participated.
- GH-1L: whether selected historical identity/context matters over a longer frozen-history flow.
- GH-2: whether current-context responsibility can causally sit between possibility formation and realization.
- GH-3: whether realized outcomes can revalidate prior governance judgments and whether the resulting feedback can causally alter a later governance epoch.

GH-3 must not reinterpret GH-1/GH-1L/GH-2 evidence or tune their rules.

## Relationship to later integration
Primary GH-3 intentionally excludes newly committed CE decision reuse. A later integrated experiment may combine:

`selective re-participation + responsibility + outcome revalidation feedback + newly completed experience reuse`

but that integrated claim must not be back-projected onto GH-3.
