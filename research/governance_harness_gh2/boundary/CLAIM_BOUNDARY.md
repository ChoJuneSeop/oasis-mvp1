# Stage 6 — Differentiation and claim boundary

## What GH-2 may test
- responsibility is recomputed from current relational context;
- the same candidate set can lead to different selections solely because current U/I/V/T context changes;
- responsibility can causally bind realization rather than remain a record-only annotation;
- candidate identity matters to responsibility profiles;
- stale responsibility can be distinguished from current responsibility;
- selected and nonselected obligations remain auditable.

## What GH-2 may not claim
- a universal moral theory;
- legal or human blame allocation;
- general AI safety superiority;
- general OASIS superiority;
- live-CARLA deployment validity;
- outcome-based responsibility correction;
- novelty over all prior art;
- that the frozen burden-set operationalization is the final mathematical form of OASIS responsibility.

## Paper/patent separation
The paper may report the causal experiment and its bounded result. Patent analysis may separately examine whether the ordered governance mechanism and provenance structure distinguish over prior art. Neither track may convert an experimental result into an unsupported novelty conclusion.
