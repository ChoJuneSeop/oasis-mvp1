## Research question
Does OASIS Ω online dependency-grounded possibility construction causally change later external reality when compared with atomic-only action selection, a frozen pre-enumerated macro library, and equally numerous/equally deep independently executable long-sequence shams?

## Hypotheses
H0: At valid online-novel decision-relevant onsets, Ω branch differences do not create reproducible downstream external trajectory differences beyond paired seed noise.

H1a — composition contribution: O0 intact online Ω and O1 atomic-only produce reproducible external trajectory divergence.

H1b — online-generation specificity: O0 and O2 frozen-macro produce reproducible divergence at onsets containing at least one admissible O0 composite absent from the frozen first-current macro library.

H1c — not generic sequence expansion: the O0 pattern is not reproduced to the same extent by O3 count+depth-matched independent-sequence sham.

No directional prediction such as faster civilization, more cooperation, or better performance is preregistered.

Each actor freezes its composite structural signature library on its first Ω call. It is immutable. The signature may encode ordered capability/action identities and dependency-kind pattern, but no outcome/reward/civilization/future/preference information.

## Decision rules
- O0 vs O1 reproducible external divergence: support for causal contribution of Ω composition in this environment.
- O0 vs O2 reproducible divergence at online-novel gates: support for online-generative necessity beyond frozen macro library.
- O0 vs O2 equivalence: frozen macro library sufficient here; online generation not shown necessary.
- O3 reproduces O0: generic long-sequence/candidate expansion is sufficient explanation; Ω specificity not supported.
- valid O0=O1: bounded null.
- accidental removal of non-Ω flow components: implementation-invalid/decomposition-induced failure, not theory falsification.

## Claim boundary
Even a positive result does not prove OASIS global superiority. It supports only the causal necessity/specificity of Ω under the frozen environment and operationalization.
