# OASIS Causal Research System v0.1

Status: research baseline
Date: 2026-09-07

## 1. Core relational process

OASIS causal research uses the following baseline process:

Past relational structure + current reality
→ possibility composition
→ single realization
→ new relational experience
→ incorporation into past relational structure
→ formation of a new relational structure
→ new possibility composition

A realized possibility becomes an actual experience and is incorporated into the accumulated past relational structure. The relational structure is not treated as being wholly overwritten after each realization.

Unrealized possibilities are not preserved as independent future paths or parallel realized worlds. They are possibilities that existed at that current moment but were not realized.

## 2. Causal research target

Causality is not reduced to a single statement of the form “cause A produced result B.”

The research target is how an intervention on a cause changes the long-horizon sequence of:

possibility composition
→ single realization
→ incorporation of realized relational experience
→ formation of subsequent relational structure
→ subsequent possibility composition.

## 3. Primary empirical layer

Primary evidence comes from observable realized flow.

For each intervention condition, record and compare:
- realized events and actions,
- realized relational experiences,
- first divergence time,
- magnitude of divergence,
- persistence duration,
- reconvergence or non-reconvergence,
- long-horizon downstream effects.

Do not infer absence of causality merely because no immediate next-step difference appears.

## 4. Internal explanatory layer

OASIS internal structures are used to explain observed causal differences, including:
- possibility compositions,
- relational reactivation,
- participant state,
- responsibility control,
- structural expansion.

Internal possibility structures must not be conflated with externally observed realized outcomes.

Unrealized possibilities are not treated as persistent unrealized paths. They are possibility compositions available at a given present but not realized at that time.

## 5. Research order

1. Define a controlled causal intervention.
2. Observe the realized flow under each condition.
3. Measure long-horizon divergence and persistence.
4. Analyze only the internal OASIS structures necessary to explain the observed difference.
5. Repeat across matched conditions, seeds, or controlled counterfactual simulations.
6. Introduce quantitative causal metrics after empirical observables are stable.

Full internal field metrics are not a prerequisite for the first causal experiments.

## 6. v0.1 research principle

The first OASIS causal metric should be grounded in how a causal intervention changes realized relational accumulation and the subsequent formation of relational structure over time, rather than in exhaustive enumeration of branching future trees.

This document supersedes any wording that describes each realization as rewriting the entire relational structure or that treats unrealized possibilities as persistent parallel future paths.
