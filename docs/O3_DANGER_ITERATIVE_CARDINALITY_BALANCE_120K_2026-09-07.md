## Interpretation

This result weakens the explanation that the O3-choice/danger association is produced only by imbalance in the observed structural covariates included in this design. It does not prove that danger is the OASIS Responsibility Axis and does not establish a causal effect of danger.

A major remaining design uncertainty is match/subsample choice: multiple equally admissible balanced control selections may yield different residual danger contrasts. Therefore a single balanced solution is insufficient as a final robustness claim.

## Next falsification target

Generate many danger-blind matched subsamples satisfying the same exact-stratum and structural-balance criteria, freeze all designs before reading danger, then measure the distribution of residual danger effects across the feasible design multiverse.

If the sign or magnitude changes substantially across equally admissible solutions, the single-solution result is design-sensitive. If the positive residual persists across diverse balanced solutions, match-selection uncertainty becomes a weaker alternative explanation.
