### What is now supported

The broad latent-store's redundancy cannot be explained solely by repeated copies of the same episode footprint.

Two layers coexist:
1. repeated episode identities inside one behaviorally equivalent relation footprint; and
2. distinct relation keys that are independently sufficient for the same current decision/target in a substantial subset of moments.

### Critical boundary

Distinct relation keys are not yet distinct causal mechanisms.

Production relation-field can collapse different relation identities into the same downstream boolean or ranking channel. Therefore cross-key overdetermination may be **functional degeneracy** rather than genuinely different decision pathways.

`functional degeneracy` = 서로 다른 내부 상태나 경로가 동일한 기능적 효과를 만드는 현상.

The next required experiment must compare downstream decision-channel traces under each independently sufficient relation key.

## Current causal research rules

1. Analyze current-flow process traces, not fixed coordinate values.
2. Preserve semantic distinctions: stored / retrieved / currentized / participated / changed decision signature / changed resolved target / changed outcome / future rewrite.
3. Actual choice uses resolved production target semantics, not internal `hidden:*` tokens.
4. Shadow evaluations reproduce production pre-evaluation transitions and fully restore state.
5. Episode identity multiplicity must be separated from relation-structure multiplicity.
6. Relation-key multiplicity must be separated from downstream mechanism multiplicity.
7. Same-current sufficiency is not longitudinal actual causation.
8. Joint contribution is not automatically unique causation.
9. Failed and zero-eligible experiments are retained; no post-hoc forcing.
10. Every new experiment requires prior-art/necessity kill-search and frozen scope.
11. Canonical and auxiliary mechanism tests remain separated.
12. Generalization remains last.
