# OASIS Causal Evidence Ledger v1.5

Date: 2026-09-07
Current semantic baseline: OASIS Causal Research System v1.0
Supersedes for current interpretation: `CAUSAL_EVIDENCE_LEDGER_v1.4.md`
Historical ledgers and negative results remain preserved unchanged.

## Semantic migration

All prior evidence remains evidence of the events actually measured, but old `future relation rewrite` wording is no longer used as a system-level interpretation.

Under causal research system v1.0:
- `outcome` = realized event boundary.
- increase in actual relation history after an outcome = realized relational experience incorporated into accumulated past relational structure.
- `field-spiral` = post-realization change in later judgment conditions.
- neither event means that the whole relational structure was overwritten.
- unrealized possibilities are not interpreted as persistent parallel future paths.

## Inherited empirical status

Preserved from prior ledgers:
- relation/process and sequence history can matter within tested scope: SUPPORTED.
- non-currentness and contextual reactivation are implementation-observed.
- process identity can be traced through participation and linked outcomes in the canonical harness.
- individual causal attribution remains distinct from joint-set attribution.
- full production noncommutativity remains UNVALIDATED.
- unrealized possibility direct causal force remains UNVALIDATED.
- danger is not equated with responsibility.
- same-current equivalence does not establish long-horizon causal equivalence.

The broad CR-01 audit historically reported zero `field-spiral` events. In v1.0 terms this means: no post-realization judgment-condition change was observed through that event channel in that particular horizon. It does **not** mean that a whole-structure rewrite failed to occur, because whole-structure rewrite is not part of v1.0 semantics.

## v1.0 evidence targets

Evidence should now be graded separately for:
1. realized outcome observed
2. newly realized relational experience incorporated into actual relation history
3. subsequent relational-structure/judgment formation changed
4. paired intervention produced realized-flow divergence
5. divergence delay measured
6. persistence measured
7. reconvergence or non-reconvergence measured
8. accumulated relational effect replicated
9. downstream long-horizon effect replicated
10. generalization outside the current harness

## Current boundaries

- Internal possibility composition is explanatory structure, not direct observation of unrealized futures.
- Shadow/counterfactual branches are analysis-only.
- Horizon non-divergence is right-censored, not proof of permanent equivalence.
- A same-current decision difference is not by itself proof of long-horizon realized causal effect.
- A realized outcome followed by relation-history growth is evidence of incorporation, not proof that every downstream change was caused by that one episode.

## Next evidence priority

The next causal work should use paired or otherwise controlled forward trajectories to measure:
- divergence delay
- realized-flow difference
- persistence
- reconvergence
- accumulated realized-relation difference
- downstream long-horizon effect

Internal OASIS traces are then used to explain where and why the observed flows diverged.
